# Words and phrases that read generic

These words appear far more in generic text than in text with something to say. None of them is banned. A single word is never proof of anything. Density is the tell: when several turn up in one paragraph, the paragraph is probably empty.

**Swapping words fixes nothing on its own.** "We leverage data to drive growth" becomes "We use data to grow", which is shorter and just as empty. The real fix is the fact the sentence was avoiding: which data, what grew, by how much. Use the swaps below when the sentence already has its fact.

## Big words for small actions

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| leverage, utilize | use |
| facilitate, enable | help, let, make possible (or say what happens) |
| embark on, commence | start |
| delve into | look at, dig into, or just start |
| endeavor | try |
| ascertain | find out, check |
| optimize | make faster, cheaper or shorter (say which) |
| implement | build, add, set up |
| streamline | cut a step (say which) |
| foster, cultivate | build, grow, encourage |
| navigate (a problem) | deal with, get through |
| unlock, unleash | (say what becomes possible) |

## Hype adjectives and intensifiers

`robust`, `seamless`, `cutting-edge`, `state-of-the-art`, `powerful`, `innovative`, `revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `comprehensive`, `holistic`, `dynamic`, `transformative`, `next-generation`, `world-class`, `best-in-class`, `crucial`, `vital`, `pivotal`, `essential`, `truly`, `incredibly`, `deeply`, `profoundly`.

**Instead:** the fact that would make the adjective true. "Seamless sync" becomes "edits show up on your phone within a second". If there's no such fact, drop the adjective and see whether the sentence still says anything.

## Stock openers

`In today's fast-paced world`, `In the ever-evolving landscape of`, `In this day and age`, `Now more than ever`, `It's no secret that`, `Let's dive in`, `Let's explore`, `When it comes to`, `Picture this:`, `Imagine a world where`, `Have you ever wondered`, `As we all know`.

**Instead:** delete the opener and start with the second sentence. Or start with the moment, the number or the reader's problem.

## Glue phrases

`ever-evolving landscape`, `rapidly changing world`, `holistic approach`, `multifaceted`, `a myriad of`, `a plethora of`, `a testament to`, `serves as a`, `stands as a`, `plays a crucial role in`, `in the realm of`, `the intricacies of`, `the tapestry of`, `a rich tapestry`, `at the intersection of`, `paving the way for`.

**Instead:** cut them. "Plays a crucial role in" is usually just "matters to", or a verb that says how: "keeps", "decides", "blocks".

## Transitions as filler

`Moreover`, `Furthermore`, `Additionally`, `That said`, `Ultimately`, `Notably`, `Importantly`, `It's worth noting that`, `It's important to remember that`, `Needless to say`.

**Instead:** delete the transition and read the two sentences together. If they still follow, the word was filler. If they don't, the logic needs fixing, not a connector.

## The staged reveal

`The result?`, `The best part?`, `Here's the thing:`, `Here's why:`, `The catch?`, `But here's the kicker`, `Spoiler:`, `Plot twist:`.

**Instead:** just say it. One of these in a whole piece can be voice. One per paragraph is a tic.

## Business-speak

`move the needle`, `low-hanging fruit`, `circle back`, `deep dive`, `synergy`, `value-add`, `take it to the next level`, `best practices`, `actionable insights`, `drive engagement`, `thought leadership`, `scalable solution`, `key stakeholders`, `win-win`.

**Instead:** say who does what. "Drive engagement" becomes "get more people to reply".

## Closers

`In conclusion`, `In summary`, `To sum up`, `At the end of the day`, `The bottom line is`, `All in all`, `Only time will tell`, `The future is bright`, `The possibilities are endless`, `And that's the real lesson`.

**Instead:** end on the last real point, an open question, or what you'd do next.

## Coaching words

`journey`, `empower`, `embrace`, `thrive`, `unlock your potential`, `you've got this`, `give yourself grace`, `it's okay to`, `self-care`, `mindset`, `growth`, `authentic self`, `show up for`.

**Instead:** in a how-to or support text, remove them and answer the question. In a text actually about wellbeing, use them only where you mean them precisely.

## Other languages

Each language has its own list. In Swedish, for example, watch for "i dagens snabbrörliga värld", "ta det till nästa nivå", "spännande resa", "skapa mervärde". In German, "in der heutigen schnelllebigen Zeit", "auf das nächste Level heben", "Mehrwert schaffen". The test is the same: does the phrase fit any topic? Then it says nothing about this one.

## Keeping the list honest

Habits change, and words drop in and out of fashion. Check the current version of a maintained list, such as [SlopDetector's word list](https://slopdetector.org/blog/ai-words-list), rather than treating this page as complete. And never use a list to reject a word the author chose on purpose.
