# Voice

The post is the owner's, written from the session. If the project rules name a reference post, read it before writing and match it. These are the defaults.

## Who is talking

- **First person, the owner.** "I", or "we" when the project rules say so.
- **The AI is a character,** not the narrator: "my AI suggested…", "the AI got this wrong". Quote it when it said something sharp or something wrong. Don't let it take credit for the owner's decisions.
- **The owner's own words are the interview.** Lift them from the session, in quotes. They carry the post. Light trims are fine; rewrites are not.

## How it sounds

- **Concrete beats general.** The actual error text, the actual number, the before and after. A table when comparing things.
- **Dry humour, no hype.** No "game-changer", "supercharge", "seamless", "unlock".
- **Honest.** What broke, what took three tries, what the AI confidently got wrong.
- **Short sentences.** No em-dashes: start a new sentence or use a comma.
- **They/them** for anyone whose pronouns aren't known.

## Shape of a section

A heading that says what happened ("The list leaked drafts", not "Discovery"), then the moment, then what it taught. One idea per section.

## The outro

Every post ends with "what happened, how it was solved, how else it could be done". A reader on another stack should leave knowing which approach suits them:

| Approach | Better at | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| What we did | … | … |
| Another way | … | … |

Then one closing line they can use.
